# **App Name**: ProSeU

## Core Features:

- AI-powered Situation Analysis: Utilize an AI tool to help users understand their legal situation based on a free-form text description and suggest initial steps or relevant learning paths.
- Interactive Learning Modules: Access structured micro-lessons, courses, and educational content on legal topics and courtroom procedures, allowing users to learn at their own pace.
- Digital Case File Builder: Tool for users to systematically organize facts, identify parties involved, track deadlines, and gather relevant documents for their legal cases.
- Legal Reference Library: Browse and search through a comprehensive catalog of law, legal fields, study modules, and specific local regulations pertinent to various jurisdictions.
- Public Case Information Search: Functionality to search for public legal case information, providing users with context and examples related to their learning or case building.
- User Authentication & Profiles: Enable secure user login, personalized profiles, and tracking of learning progress and enrolled programs or certifications.
- Legal News Aggregator: Provide a feed of curated legal news and updates on recent legal precedents to keep users informed about current events in the legal world.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep professional purple (#342E55) for gravitas and authority, forming a strong contrast with the light background. HSL(252, 30%, 25%).
- Background color: Very light off-white with a subtle purple undertone (#F9F7FB) to maintain a clean, open, and inviting aesthetic. HSL(260, 10%, 97%).
- Accent color: A clear and bright blue (#4D9DE0) for interactive elements, providing visual cues for action and representing clarity and trust. HSL(222, 70%, 55%).
- Headings and body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its clean, modern, and highly legible characteristics, suitable for an educational and professional platform.
- Simple, outline-style icons with rounded edges, maintaining a contemporary and approachable look consistent with the educational focus.
- A spacious, grid-based layout utilizing card-like components to present information clearly and facilitate intuitive navigation, with a prominent central section for key interactions.
- Subtle, smooth transition effects for elements like button hovers, section changes, and content loading to enhance user engagement and visual fluidity.
