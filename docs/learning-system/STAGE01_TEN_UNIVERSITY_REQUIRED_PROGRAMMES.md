## Nonredundancy rules

1. **One legal learning objective → one primary ProSeU home.** Multiple universities can link to the same destination when their *specific* objectives truly match.
2. Retain secondary links where an objective needs reinforcement elsewhere, without copying the entire explanatory unit.
3. Preserve jurisdictional boundaries and differences. Do not collapse US federal procedure, Singapore common law, English legal procedure, Roman historical sources, and civil-law codes into one supposedly universal rule.
4. Identify required / elective / choose-one / embedded-skill status in original programme. An elective cannot become mandatory simply to inflate coverage.
5. Associate a source objective with exact official syllabus URL, academic year, course/module, learning outcome, ProSeU lesson IDs and tested question IDs, plus independent reviewer.
6. A title-to-title crosswalk and this reference register **do not certify** the complete ten-university curriculum.

## Educational quality gate

A lesson is review-ready only when its red→yellow→orange→black progression adds real knowledge, 3–5 narrative sections connect without filler, quizzes test application and explanations, sources are accurate, and jurisdiction-specific propositions are qualified.
