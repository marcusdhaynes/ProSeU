# PSU-1.1 — Major Cases: primary sources, case scope, and academic review

**Editorial status:** 28 original draft lessons across 7 subsections × 4 mastery levels. Completed authoring of the competency is **not** permission to publish. No affiliation with the named courts or universities is implied. Date: 2026-11-04.

## How to use this map

The authoritative materials are the decisions, not a quotation from a secondary summary. Each case is introduced through facts and the issue actually resolved, then its holding, limits and later significance. Read at least the operative portions of the decision before academic sign-off. The four levels are separate perspectives rather than four copies of the same abstract:

- **Red / Paralegal:** recognize the parties, facts, holding, and human interest involved.
- **Yellow / Bachelor:** distinguish the legal rule and its procedural or doctrinal limits.
- **Orange / Masters:** compare another authority, competing opinions or a later qualification.
- **Black / Doctorate:** defend and critique institutional, moral, and comparative implications.

Files in `lessons/stage-01/PSU-1.1/major_cases_*.json` contain summary, focus, three outcome checkboxes, three reading sections per tier with matched read-aloud scripts and optional video fields, and eight assessments per tier. All content has `status: "draft"`.

## Primary judgment and institutional source map

| Case / year | Source (decision, court or official institution) | Principle actually taught | Scope or limitation requiring review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marbury v Madison** (1803) | [5 U.S. 137 — Supreme Court opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/5/137) | Written Constitution limits the Court's original jurisdiction; judicial review | Do not claim Madison was ordered to deliver the commission; avoid claiming every constitution uses identical review |
| **Miller (No 1)** (2017) | [UK Supreme Court, [2017] UKSC 5](https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2016-0196) | Parliamentary authorization required for triggering Article 50 in the circumstances | Distinguish lawful authority to initiate withdrawal from its political merits |
| **Brown v Board of Education** (1954) | [U.S. National Archives — judgment](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/brown-v-board-of-education) | State-mandated racial segregation in public schools violates equal protection | Distinguish the 1954 holding from the contested 1955 remedial implementation |
| **Grootboom** (2000) | [South African Constitutional Court, [2000] ZACC 19](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZACC/2000/19.html) | Housing measures must be reasonable and address urgent needs | Section 26 does not guarantee the immediate provision of any particular home |
| **Entick v Carrington** (1765) | [King's Bench, judgment](https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/KB/1765/J98.html) | Executive intrusion into home and papers must have legal authority | Do not translate into a universal contemporary warrant test |
| **Youngstown Sheet & Tube Co v Sawyer** (1952) | [343 U.S. 579 — Supreme Court opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/343/579) | Truman lacked authority for the steel-mill seizure on asserted grounds | Justice Jackson's three categories are an influential concurrence, not the majority's exact opinion |
| **Gideon v Wainwright** (1963) | [372 U.S. 335 — Supreme Court opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/372/335) | States must provide counsel for indigent defendants in felony criminal proceedings | Do not expand the holding to every civil or administrative dispute |
| **Miranda v Arizona** (1966) | [384 U.S. 436 — Supreme Court opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/384/436) | Warnings and safeguards for custodial interrogation under the Fifth Amendment | Subsequent cases qualify custody, interrogation, waiver and evidentiary remedies |
| **Salduz v Turkey** (2008) | [ECHR Grand Chamber, application 36391/02](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=001-89893) | As a rule, early access to counsel during police interrogation under Convention Article 6 | See [Ibrahim and Others v UK](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=002-11189) (2016) on exceptional restrictions and overall fairness |
| **Donoghue v Stevenson** (1932) | [UK House of Lords, [1932] AC 562](https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKHL/1932/100.html) | Possible manufacturer duty of care to a consumer without direct contract | Alleged snail and negligence were not finally proven by the duty decision |
| **MacPherson v Buick Motor Co** (1916) | [New York Court of Appeals, 217 N.Y. 382](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/court-of-appeals/1916/217-n-y-382-1916.html) | Manufacturer negligence duty can extend to foreseeable users beyond contractual privity | Not a rule of absolute manufacturer liability for any defect |
| **Fairchild v Glenhaven** (2002) | [UK House of Lords, [2002] UKHL 22](https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKHL/2002/22.html) | Exceptional material-risk route in multiple negligent asbestos-exposure mesothelioma cases | Not a universal relaxation of causation for all statistical associations |
| **Palsgraf v Long Island Railroad** (1928) | [New York Court of Appeals, 248 N.Y. 339](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/court-of-appeals/1928/248-n-y-339-1928.html) | Cardozo's plaintiff-relative duty and foreseeable-risk limitation | Contrast Andrews's dissent; do not confuse duty analysis with unlimited remote causation |
| **Caparo Industries plc v Dickman** (1990) | [UK House of Lords, [1990] UKHL 2](https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKHL/1990/2.html) | Limits on claimed auditor duties to investors relying on accounts | The three considerations are not a universal negligence algorithm |
| **Robinson v Chief Constable** (2018) | [UK Supreme Court, [2018] UKSC 4](https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2016-0082) | Ordinary negligence principles may govern police officers' positive actions | No universal police immunity; no blanket duty to prevent all third-party crime |
| **Mabo v Queensland (No 2)** (1992) | [High Court of Australia, native title — Attorney-General's Department](https://www.ag.gov.au/legal-system/native-title) and [AIATSIS case record](https://aiatsis.gov.au/ntpd-resource/742) | Australian common law recognizes qualifying Indigenous native title grounded in traditional law and custom | Title may be affected by valid extinguishment; not automatic transfer of every parcel |
| **Van Gend en Loos** (1963) | [Court of Justice — fundamental cases](https://curia.europa.eu/site/jcms/qua1_5960/en/fundamental-cases-at-the-court-of-justice) | Direct effect of qualifying Community law provisions for individuals | Do not assume every treaty obligation is directly enforceable |
| **Costa v ENEL** (1964) | [Court of Justice — fundamental cases](https://curia.europa.eu/site/jcms/qua1_5960/en/fundamental-cases-at-the-court-of-justice) | Primacy of Community law over conflicting national law within scope | Keep primacy distinct from individual direct effect and treaty competences |
| **Kesavananda Bharati** (1973) | [Supreme Court of India — basic structure judgment portal](https://judgments.ecourts.gov.in/KBJ/?p=home) | Amendment power does not permit destruction of the Constitution's basic structure | Amendment power still exists; scope of basic features requires precise doctrinal analysis |
