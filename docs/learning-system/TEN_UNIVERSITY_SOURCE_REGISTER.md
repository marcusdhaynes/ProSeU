## Evidence-complete standard

A source objective is verified only when the team records:
1. The university and **specific required course or qualifying requirement**, its cohort and an official detailed syllabus/learning-outcomes citation;
2. The concept, analytical skill, case or doctrine being taught and its jurisdictional scope;
3. The canonical `M-` entry (or documented replacement), and whether its learning objective duplicates another source objective;
4. Primary ProSeU destination `PSU-stage.course / competency / subsection / mastery level`, plus any necessary secondary link;
5. The lesson section(s) teaching it, question(s) testing it, reviewer, date and outcome.

A crosswalk should detect uncovered source objectives, unsupported claims, duplicate destinations, outdated legal rules, and content taught only at a level too shallow for the intended mastery. **A programme-level page alone is not an objective-level verification.**

## Scope of this package

The 28 newly authored Jurisprudence/Major Doctrines lessons are **draft teaching content**. This document documents the evidence standard and reference list, not a claim that all ten academic programmes have been merged, deduplicated, or mastered.
