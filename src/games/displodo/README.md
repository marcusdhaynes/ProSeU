# Displodo

A ship and drifting rocks. Each rock carries an answer. Shooting (click, tap, or Enter on the highlighted rock) a right answer scores and loads the next prompt. A wrong rock costs a life and does not advance. The typed field grades fill-in and short answers. The clock and drift speed follow the level position.

Content: `template_id` `displodo`, `questionType` `asteroids`, with `prompt`, `correct`, and `distractors`. `options` plus `correctIndex` also works. `submitAnswer` uses the template name `Displodo`.
