# Pecu Advo

An original field study on a green pocket screen. The creatures (Quillfox, Sealbear, Docketowl, Writnewt, Gavelpup, Lexmoth, Briefbat) are not from any other game. Areas are the seven competencies, in catalog order. Walking onto a creature opens a turn: pick or type the answer. A correct answer clears that creature. The house mark leaves the area once its creatures are done, which opens the next area. Composure (hearts) follows the level position.

Content is the question list at that level, grouped by competency id or subsection id. A `pecu-advo` item is included; so are ordinary true/false, multiple choice, matching, and fill-in items. `submitAnswer` uses the template name `Pecu Advo` and the subsection stored on the question.
